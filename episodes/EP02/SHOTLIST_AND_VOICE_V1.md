### Timing and accuracy notes
- Total is **135sec in this initial allocation**; intended to be **trimmed to ~120–125sec** after a natural-voice read-through. Don't speed speech up to fit the planned beats.
- The syringe analogy should not imply the mechanical power is created by oil. Larger piston force comes with displacement/travel tradeoffs in an ideal hydraulic arrangement.
- Pressure is not solely set by pump; in a typical circuit it rises with opposing load and is limited/controlled by safety/control devices.
- Hydraulic cylinder is not always 'pushed out' by oil in all motions. Depending on valve/chamber, piston extends or retracts.
- No misleading numbers or 'lifts any tonnage'. If using calculations, display p, A, F only, not arbitrary real-machine pressure.
- Capy and engineer remain consistent with EP01 and approved sonic logo.
