## Dialogue / speech workflow
1. Lock exact Vietnamese dialogue in the script files before voice generation.
2. Use CapCut text-to-speech to audition **2–3 legal Vietnamese AI voices** for Capy. Character voice: energetic, friendly, lightly comedic; avoid shrill voice or imitation of recognizable people.
3. Owner records own narration on phone/USB microphone for engineer/explainer, dry quiet room, no background music; preferably 48 kHz WAV.

5. Timeline: separate stems CAPY_AI.wav / NARRATOR_OWNER.wav / SFX.wav / MUSIC.wav and subtitle SRT; sound effects - avoid masking intelligible speech.
