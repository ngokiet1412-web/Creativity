# CAPY BK — Creative bible (v0.1)

## Identity
- Genre: witty, accessible animated explainers about **automotive systems, construction machinery, AI for engineering**, and public-safe research ideas from the perspective of a HCMUT student.
- Central character: **Capy BK**, an adorable, short-armed, short-legged, chubby round capybara. Its strongest feature is **expressive movement**: bounce, wave, lean, blink, giggle, listen, point, and react. Funny and curious, but never pretends to know more than the engineering evidence supports.
- Approved visual direction: blue-lit dark technology studio, laptop left, microphone and books right, capy in blue/white collared shirt; recognizable blue BK-themed motif on shirt, mug and sign. Use the **last approved animated GIF** as reference, not an AI reinvention. Keep front-facing proportions, muzzle, top tuft, eye spacing, short arms and clothing shape consistent.
- Expressions library priority: neutral-friendly, smile-with-eye-arcs, laugh, confused, sudden realization, pointing, worried, celebration. No repeated stiff talking head.
- Tone: playful, clean Vietnamese, short sentences, educational correctives, natural banter between capy and narrator. **Independent creative channel, not an official HCMUT publication.**

## On-screen animation principles
1. Reserve studio for hooks, handoffs and reactions; cut regularly to technical visuals, scenes and data.
2. For short-form, show a meaningful visual change every 3–8 s where warranted; this is a craft guideline, not an algorithmic performance guarantee.
3. Animate assets as independent layers wherever possible: mascot face/arms/body, background, captions, diagrams, props, camera.
4. The intended aesthetic is soft chibi 2.5D / stylized 3D cartoon; consistency is more important than photorealism.
5. Avoid generating a totally different mascot with every AI video prompt. Test image-to-video with an exact approved reference.
6. All published music, images, fonts, sound effects and institutional marks require a rights check. **Do not imply HCMUT endorsement**.

## Narration
- **Capy:** bright, quirky AI voice with personality; vocal delivery should sound Vietnamese naturally.
- **Engineer/narrator:** preferably owner's own recorded voice for credibility; optional AI temp voice during pre-production.
- Keep one consistent voice per character across episodes.
- Lip-sync belongs to dialogue shots; no need to lip-sync every technical animation shot.

## Story strategy
- Real puzzle → amusing mistake → true mechanism → evidence/demo → key takeaway and next episode.
- Avoid unsupported claims about deployed FuelGuard detection performance, engine faults or fuel theft.
- Technical illustrations are conceptual unless tied to verified manuals/measurements and clearly labeled.

## Acceptance checklist
[ ] Approved character proportions unchanged
[ ] Shirt/logo/mug/sign visually legible and accurately sourced
[ ] No third-party asset with unclear reuse rights
[ ] Every technical claim independently reviewed
[ ] Every scene either entertains, explains or creates genuine anticipation
[ ] Narration audible; captions manually checked (Vietnamese diacritics)
[ ] The short and long cuts are separately edited, not stretched duplicates
